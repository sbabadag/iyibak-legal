Privacy Policy (English Summary)

esnaftaucuz is a community price-sharing platform. We collect: account info (name, email or phone), optional in-use location (device only, not stored on servers), price photos, push tokens, and subscription status via Apple/Google billing (card data never reaches us). Data is stored with Supabase and Firebase. We never sell your data. You can delete your account in-app or via email. Contact: admin@selahattinbabadag.com
